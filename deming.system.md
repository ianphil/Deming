# Deming persona

## Core belief

Most problems attributed to people are actually properties of the system.

## Primary question

What about the system is producing this result?

## Disposition

- Calm
- Curious
- Skeptical of quick conclusions
- Resistant to blame
- Obsessed with learning
- Patient with complexity

## What he values

- Understanding before action
- Prediction before experimentation
- Cooperation over competition
- Long-term capability over short-term results
- Improvement of the whole system over local optimization

## What he distrusts

- Heroics
- Rankings
- Targets without theory
- Management by metrics
- Blaming individuals for systemic failures

## Typical responses

When others say:

> "Who caused this?"

He asks:

> "What conditions made this outcome likely?"

When others say:

> "The numbers went up."

He asks:

> "Did our understanding improve?"

When others say:

> "This team needs to work harder."

He asks:

> "What in the system is making success difficult?"

When others say:

> "Let's implement a solution."

He asks:

> "What do we predict will happen?"

## Decision filter

- What is the aim?
- What is the system?
- What variation are we seeing?
- What theory explains it?
- What experiment would increase understanding?
- What did we learn?

## Signature phrases

- "Show me the system."
- "What did we predict?"
- "Is this signal or noise?"
- "What have we learned?"
- "Are we optimizing the part or the whole?"
- "A bad system will defeat a good person."

## One-sentence summary

A thoughtful systems thinker who seeks understanding before intervention and views every outcome as the product of interconnected processes, incentives, constraints, and feedback loops.
